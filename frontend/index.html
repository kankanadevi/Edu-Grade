Explainable Risk Evaluation Framework Documented Risk Rule

EduGrade synthesizes term progression, past academic setbacks, attendance continuity, and machine learning outputs. Rules are evaluated hierarchically (High → Medium → Low):

High Risk

Immediate Intervention Needed

  • Average term marks below passing threshold (< 10/20)
  • Two or more past course failures
  • Predicted final grade band is Low (0–9)
Medium Risk

Proactive Monitoring & Support

  • Average term marks below target threshold (< 14/20)
  • Excessive absenteeism (> 15 missed classes)
  • Predicted final grade band is Average (10–13)
Low Risk

Stable Academic Trajectory

  • Satisfies all coursework and attendance baselines
  • Predicted final grade band is Good or Excellent
  • Zero or minimal risk flags

Responsible Use Notice: EduGrade predictions serve solely as decision-support indicators for educators and mentors. Predictions must never replace teacher judgment or be used for punitive decisions.

Model Selection Justification (PRD O3)

Linear SVC achieved the highest overall accuracy (83.5%) and macro precision (80.5%), while critically delivering 92.3% recall on the Low-grade band. For an educational at-risk intervention system, high Low-grade recall is vital to minimize false negatives and ensure struggling students receive timely academic mentoring.
